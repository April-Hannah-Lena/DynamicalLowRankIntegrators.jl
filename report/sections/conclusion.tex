\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We have presented a dynamical low-rank integrator for the Vlasov-Poisson equation 
and have shown that it can obey arbitrarily many local conservation equations via 
the choice of an appropriate fixed portion of the dynamical basis. Moreover, the 
derived error representation confirms a well-known property of many integrators, 
namely that the error in an observable is often significantly lower than the 
pessimistic estimate which one often achieves in the numerical analysis of various 
integration schemes.
